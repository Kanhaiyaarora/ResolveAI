COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------
ResolveAI provides advanced artificial intelligence solutions for customer service teams, helping businesses automate support tickets, live chat, knowledge base generation, and customer insights. The platform integrates with CRM systems, helpdesk software, and communication channels to improve customer satisfaction while reducing operational costs.

Mission:
To transform customer support through intelligent automation, making support faster, smarter, and more scalable.

Vision:
To become the leading AI customer support infrastructure for B2B enterprises globally.
